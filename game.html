How to Play Loop Quest

Welcome to Loop Lab! Help Looper navigate 4 robotics lab worlds (20 levels) by mastering Python:

  • Track & Lives: Each level has 5 puzzles and 3 Energy Cells. Correct answers hop Looper forward and earn XP & coins; wrong answers cost 1 Energy Cell.
  • 5 Puzzle Types: Predict output, tap the buggy line, fill keyword blanks, reorder code lines (drag or tap arrows), and count loop runs.
  • Loop Coach AI: Stuck? Spend 1 coin for up to 3 progressive hints, including a live line-by-line simulator walkthrough!
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: 1–4 select options, Enter checks/advances, H opens a hint, M mutes audio.
